CASE 01 · BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

Apple Ecosystem Customer Retention

Turn 100,000 synthetic customer records into a retention intelligence system that helps explain who churns, what drives churn, and where ecosystem engagement can be strengthened.

PRIMARY OUTCOME84.53%retention rate
01BUSINESS QUESTION

Who is leaving, and why?

Frame retention as a business problem around churn, engagement, customer satisfaction, support burden, device ownership and subscription behaviour.

02DATA

100K customer records

Clean and validate the synthetic customer dataset before analysis. Python, Pandas and NumPy handle preprocessing and exploratory analysis.

03ANALYTICS MODEL

Reusable DAX measures

Build measures for retention, churn, customer lifetime value, device ownership and ecosystem engagement so KPIs remain consistent across views.

04DECISION LAYER

Four-page Power BI story

Package the analysis into Executive Overview, Churn Deep Dive, Ecosystem Engagement and Customer Segments for drill-down analysis.

WHAT THE ANALYSIS SURFACED

The dashboard moves from “what happened?” to “what should we investigate?”

Key churn drivers identified in the project include device ownership, iCloud subscription tier, customer satisfaction and support ticket frequency. The output is designed around actionable retention decisions rather than visual volume.

AutoDiag AI
